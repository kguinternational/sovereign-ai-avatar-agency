### How it Works in the Field

1. **The Inbound Call:** A homeowner with a leak calls your business at 2:00 AM on a Sunday.
2. **The AI Answers:** "Hi, thanks for calling Apex Roofing. I understand you might be dealing with some storm damage. Are you experiencing an active leak right now?"
3. **The Empathy & Triage:** The AI uses advanced LLMs to converse naturally, showing empathy and collecting critical data (address, type of roof, severity of the leak).
4. **The Booking:** The AI checks your active calendar and says, "I have an inspector available tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Does that work for you?"
5. **The Handoff:** The AI books the slot, texts the homeowner a confirmation, and drops the summary directly into your CRM.
